How It Works (In 10 Seconds)

Type in any order. Typos are fixed automatically. Click any example below to test it:

1 • PAST PAPERS
Subject + Session + Year + Paper
Press Enter ↵ to open the PDF. Turn Include MS → yes to open QP & MS together.
2 • EXAMINER REPORTS
Just Add "er" or "examiner report"
No paper or variant needed. Auto-completes & checks which years have an official report.
3 • SYLLABUS PDF
Type "syllabus" or Flip the Top Toggle
Fetches the official Cambridge syllabus PDF for any O-Level or A-Level subject.
4 • UPCOMING SCHEDULE
Type Subject + Paper for Live Dates
Instantly shows upcoming Feb/Mar & May/June exam dates and days remaining.
Shortcuts: mj / s23 = May/June on / w23 = Oct/Nov fm / m23 = Feb/Mar (or Further Maths) p12 or p1v2 = Paper 1 Variant 2
